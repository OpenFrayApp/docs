A session ends mid-fight more often than it ends cleanly. **Saving** keeps the board
exactly as it stands, so the next session starts where this one stopped instead of
starting from your notes.

Saving needs an account. It has to be kept somewhere that isn't this browser, or it
wouldn't be there on the laptop you run next week's game from.

## Saving the board

The **save** button is the floppy disk in the top bar.

1. Click the **save** button. A small box opens.
2. Type a name in **Name**. Something you'll recognise next week: `Bridge fight` beats
   `Session 12`.
3. Click **Save**.

What gets kept is the board as it stands: the creatures, the party, hit points, effects,
conditions, initiative, the round you're on, and the log. It is a snapshot, not a link.
Carrying on with the fight afterwards changes nothing about what you saved, and saving
again under the same name keeps both.

You can save as many fights as you like, and one board can be saved as often as you
want.

## Finding it again

Saved fights live in the compendium, under **Encounters**.

1. Open the **compendium**.
2. Choose the **Encounters** tab.
3. Click a saved fight to read it.

Its card shows what was on the board when you saved, grouped and counted under
**Creatures**, **Allies**, and **Party**. You can tell one goblin ambush from another
without opening either. The date it was saved is on the card, and the name can be edited
in place.

## The two ways back in

The card has two buttons, and the difference matters:

- **Restore** brings the whole encounter back: the party, the hit points, the effects,
  the round, the log. This is the one for picking up where you left off. It replaces the
  board you have now.
- **Add creatures** drops only its creatures onto the board you have now, at full hit
  points, leaving the party behind. This is the one for running the same ambush again
  with a different group, or for using an old fight as prep.

**Restore** asks first, because the board you have now goes away.

**Delete** removes the saved fight. It asks first too, and it can't be undone.

:::note[Saving is not publishing]
A saved fight is yours and is visible to nobody else. If you want to hand an encounter to
another Game Master, that's a different thing with a different button: see
[Publish an encounter](/docs/guides/publishing/).
:::

## What a save doesn't hold

- **The compendium itself.** A saved fight references the creatures it used, so a
  creature you later edit comes back edited, and one you delete comes back as the
  snapshot of it that was on the board.
- **Your settings.** Player-view choices, house rules and the rest belong to your account
  and your campaign, not to a fight.
- **Anything from a browser you weren't signed in on.** A fight run signed out lives in
  that tab and ends with it.
