OpenFray is a combat console and initiative tracker for Dungeons and Dragons. It keeps
track of a fight for you: who acts when, everyone's hit points, the conditions and
effects on each creature, and the dice. It also has a built-in list of creatures and
spells to pull from. It runs in your web browser, on a laptop, a tablet, or a phone, and
you don't need an account. Signing in lets you save your game.

The handbook is organized by what you need. New here? Start with
[Getting started](/docs/getting-started/) and run your first fight. After that, the
guides give you the steps for each task, the concepts explain how OpenFray thinks, and
the reference describes every screen in detail.
